# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential collaborators evaluating Ankit Kumar for AI/ML or Full-Stack engineering roles.

## Product Purpose
A personal portfolio to showcase technical depth, specifically projects bridging AI, computer vision, and full-stack development. Success means securing interviews or collaborations.

## Positioning
A developer who builds end-to-end systems at the intersection of AI and software engineering, with a strong emphasis on local/offline LLM tooling and real-time inference (e.g., face recognition, gesture control) without relying on third-party APIs.

## Operating Context
Viewed primarily in desktop or mobile browsers by technical or semi-technical evaluators who may quickly scan for tech stack, GitHub links, and resume access.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must preserve the existing downloadable resume functionality (`/public/resume.pdf`).
- Built as a React single-page application using Vite and Tailwind CSS v4.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Ankit Kumar
- Voice: Professional, self-driven "tinkerer" who builds to learn and ships to grow.
- Assets: A dark-themed visual identity with cyan, violet, and fuchsia accents and specific typography (Sora, Inter, JetBrains Mono).

## Evidence on Hand
- Project links to GitHub (e.g., SecureVault, SignSpeak).
- Downloadable resume (`public/resume.pdf`).
- No fabricated testimonials or metrics to be added.

## Product Principles
- **Show technical depth:** Highlight the integration of frontend interfaces with complex backend/AI models (e.g., OpenCV, MediaPipe, Ollama).
- **Builder's mindset:** Emphasize shipping functional, local-first tools over generic web pages.
- **Clarity for evaluators:** Ensure skills and project links remain scannable and accessible.
